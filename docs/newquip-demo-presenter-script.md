# NewQuip POC demonstration - presenter script (connected Preview, POC Presentation Mode)

Everything here is a POC. The journey is the point, not the commercial data. Sign in once as testing@sparkconsulting.co.za (emailed code); use the Owner / Rep / Estimator / Manager buttons under the menu.

**How POC Presentation Mode works:** a quotation CREATED with **New Grower quote** while the mode is on is marked "POC PRESENTATION" and seeded with the Estimator inputs the workbook cannot supply (illustrative prices, product choices, delivery and installation). So there is nothing for the Estimator to type, no return to the Rep, and no cost / mapping / exception forms. At sign-off and approval the application itself records the normal incomplete-cost exception and the Manager's baseline exception with the reason "POC PRESENTATION ... not commercially validated" (audited). Ordinary quotations and other organisations are unaffected.

**Rep**
1. **Rep** > Solution Builder > **New Grower quote**. Step 1: search the customer (Spark), select it, choose the quotation contact.
2. Step 2: site name; **Load sample measurements** (12 rooms, 49.5 x 14.43 m, 20 pens, 600 pigs); **Save draft**.
3. Step 3: **Load recommended configuration** (or fill quantities per room), penning pig size **Small**, running metres **20**, slatted floor area **120**; **Save draft**. Do not exclude anything: this is the full configuration.
4. Review, then **Submit to Estimator** (tick the confirmation).

**Estimator / Manager / PDF**
5. **Estimator**: **Next: Final review** > **Mark reviewed & continue** > **Sign off for Manager approval**.
6. **Manager**: **Approve** (**Approve & Continue** in POC presentation mode).
7. **Rep**: Stage 4 **Generate customer quotation PDF**, **Open PDF** (DEMONSTRATION ONLY terms apply to demonstration quotations).

**Acceptance and project**
8. **Manager**: Stage 5 record customer acceptance (evidence reference + any small PDF). Stage 6 **Create Project from accepted quote**, **Open the Project**.

**Cin7 Sales Order, progress, claim, invoice (POC account, test transactions)**
9. **Owner (you)** (project administration is owner/admin only): **Commercial / Claims** > Cin7 Sales Order: location **MSTR**, tax rule **Standard Rate Sales**, revenue account **200**; confirm and **Create**. Resolve any scope line the panel lists by choosing Exclude (they are carried by the contract charges).
10. **Stages** > Deposit **Update progress**: physical 100% then **Approve commercial progress** 100%. **Commercial / Claims**: tick Deposit, **Preview claim**, **Save as draft claim**, **Submit**.
11. **Manager** > **Approvals** > open the claim > **Approve...** > **Confirm**.
12. **Owner (you)** > Project > Commercial / Claims > **Post to Cin7**. The Sale and invoice numbers show on the Project.

If an Estimator or Manager ever changes a customer-facing amount, the quotation returns to the Rep: the screen says why and offers **Continue as Rep**.
